🌌 WHY JULY 20 IS MOON DAY

On July 20, 1969, humanity achieved the impossible. The Apollo 11 Lunar Module, Eagle, landed on the Sea of Tranquility. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin took the historic first footsteps on another celestial world, declaring: "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."

July 20 is celebrated globally to honor this milestone of cosmic exploration, inspiration, and scientific progress. Today, as India's Chandrayaan missions reveal water ice resources at the lunar south pole and NASA's Artemis program prepares to send the next generation of astronauts back to the Moon, this day serves as a powerful reminder of human ingenuity and space science discovery.
